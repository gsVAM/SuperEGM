### The three respec highs

1. **#240, foreman spec finding never reaches the owner as respec: OPEN.** The foreman raises `collect --spec`, which records a high `deviation (spec) unruled:` finding.

2. **#242, a respec's scope misses units: OPEN, two parts.**

3. **#246, the wave never ends: OPEN.** Conditions:
   - a unit is clean or proceeded;
   - the unit raised a respec that is still open, not yet approved;
   - its PR reads CONFLICTING;
   - the approval is parallel (`rounds`).
